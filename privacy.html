Starlight privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Starlight is a free space game for children. We collect as little as possible, and we never sell or share it.

What children share

Nothing personal. Children play without an account. On the Top scores board a player appears only under a nickname built from the game's own word lists, such as "Happy Otter 37". Children cannot type their own name or any other text.

What we store for every player

  • A random player ID created by the game, so a player's best score can be saved.
  • That player's nickname, best scores and farthest world reached, shown on the public Top scores board.
  • Game progress (discoveries, stardust, outfits), kept in the browser on the player's own device.

If a grown-up signs in to save progress

A parent or carer can choose to sign in with an email code or a Google account so progress can continue on another device. In that case we also store:

  • The grown-up's email address, used only to sign in. It is never shown in the game or on the board.
  • A copy of the game progress, which only that account can read.

Who processes the data

Scores and accounts are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Google sign-in is handled by Google. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Removing data

To remove a player's score or a signed-in account and its saved progress, open an issue at github.com/bilzz1326/starlight/issues asking for removal, without posting any personal details there, and we will follow up. Clearing the browser's site data removes the progress kept on that device.
